# KeenWG name, artwork, and redistribution

KeenWG is open-source software distributed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The license permits use, modification, and redistribution of the code. It does not grant permission to imply that a modified build is an official release from Apex Intellect or the KeenWG project.

## Exact mirrors are welcome

You may mirror an unmodified official KeenWG APK when all of the following remain intact:

- the APK bytes and Android signature;
- the application name, package identifier, attribution, and source link;
- the published version and, where shown, its SHA-256 digest;
- a clear statement that your service is a mirror rather than the developer or official release channel.

An exact mirror will still show “Official build” inside the app because the embedded publisher certificate check verifies the APK signature, not the download website.

## Modified builds

If you distribute a modified APK, use a distinct application name, icon, package identifier, and signing key. You may describe it factually as “based on KeenWG” and link to this repository, but do not call it an official KeenWG or Apex Intellect release and do not use Apex Intellect artwork in a way that suggests endorsement.

Forks may keep the Apache-required notices and factual compatibility references. Please make user-visible modifications clear so users can distinguish your build from releases signed by Apex Intellect.

## Project identity

“KeenWG”, the KeenWG application icon, “Apex Intellect”, and Apex Intellect artwork identify the upstream project and publisher. This policy is about source identification; it does not claim registration of any mark that is not registered.

For permission beyond this policy, contact Apex Intellect through [apex-intellect.ru](https://apex-intellect.ru/) or the project maintainer [@th-notorious](https://github.com/th-notorious).
